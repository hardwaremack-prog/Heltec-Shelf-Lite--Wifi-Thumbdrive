The Shelf Lite

The Shelf, shrunk onto a Heltec LoRa board. It's a Wi-Fi file hub on a microSD card, with a music player for your phone or a little speaker.

1. Install it

Plug the board into this computer with a USB-C data cable, pick your board, then choose the serial port when Chrome asks. This works in Chrome or Edge on a PC, Mac or Raspberry Pi.

Installing Shelf Lite replaces whatever is on the board, Meshtastic included. You can put Meshtastic back any time from flasher.meshtastic.org.
Heltec V3

WiFi LoRa 32 V3

Open this page in Chrome or Edge to install. This page has to be opened over https. 8 MB flash. With no card fitted it keeps about 5.9 MB of files on the board.
Heltec V4

WiFi LoRa 32 V4 / V4-R8

Open this page in Chrome or Edge to install. This page has to be opened over https. 16 MB flash. With no card fitted it keeps about 13.9 MB on the board. Works on both the 2 MB and 8 MB PSRAM versions.

No port listed? On the V3, hold PRG, tap RST, let go of PRG and try again. The V4 shows up right away. On Windows the V3 may need the free CP210x USB driver.

2. Wire it up

One wiring plan works on the V3 and the V4. The pins stay clear of the LoRa radio, the screen, and the V4's high-power amplifier pins.

Go by the pin labels printed on the board. GPIO 47 and 48 are on the other header row from 4, 6, 38, 39 and 41, so the SD module's wires go to both sides of the board.

Keep the SD wires under 10 cm. Use a FAT32 card: 32 GB cards come that way. Bigger cards need reformatting to FAT32 first (on Windows, use a tool like guiformat). With no card fitted, Shelf Lite uses the board's own flash instead.

3. Connect it to your Wi-Fi

  1. Power the board. The screen shows Join Shelf-Lite-XXXX.
  2. On your phone, join that Wi-Fi network. The password is shelflite. A setup page pops up; if it doesn't, open http://192.168.4.1.
  3. Go to Device → Find networks, tap your home Wi-Fi and type its password. The board restarts.
  4. Put your phone back on your home Wi-Fi. Open the address on the board's screen, or http://shelf-xxxx.local.
  5. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to get a Shelf icon.

Hold the PRG button for 6 seconds to forget the Wi-Fi and go back to setup mode. A short press skips to the next song on the speaker, or wakes the screen.

What it does

📁 Files

Upload from your phone or PC, make folders, rename, delete, download. Photos, videos and text open right in the page.

🎵 Music

Folders become albums. Play on your phone (lock-screen controls work) or on the Shelf speaker, with volume control.

📌 Board

A shared board of notes and links for everyone on the home Wi-Fi.

📟 Screen

Shows the address, free space and the song that's playing. Sleeps after 5 minutes to save the OLED.
